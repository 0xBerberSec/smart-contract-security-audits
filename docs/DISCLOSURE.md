# Disclosure Policy

This selection publishes the official Injective outcome and generic descriptions of research practice. It does not publish unresolved technical findings, private source material, executable protocol PoCs or private execution traces.

Technical material requires a review of the exact source revision, original report, applicable program terms, third-party rights, remediation/publication status and authorization for the content being released. A competition result or a previous self-authored publication does not substitute for that basis.

Sensitive studies remain local. Sharing with a prospective audit team also requires an appropriate disclosure basis; recruitment is not an exception. Generic method overviews omit protocol-specific vulnerable functions, parameters and exploitation preconditions.

Earlier repository history and previously published copies are not rewritten by this preparation. A revised working tree does not retract historical publications or establish permission for their contents.
