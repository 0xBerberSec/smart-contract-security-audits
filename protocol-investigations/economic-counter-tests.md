# Economic Controls and Hypothesis Rejection

**Method overview — underlying technical case withheld.**

## Research question

Receiving more tokens than supplied in one action is not sufficient to demonstrate protocol-created value. Other actors, previous transfers, legitimate rewards and pricing effects can fund the apparent gain.

## Approach

I track the participants and origin of the assets, compare before-and-after positions, and separate a local effect from a feasible attacker strategy. I identify what the experiment excludes, such as execution costs or a complete market model.

## Counter-tests and interpretation

A control removes the suspected precondition or changes one variable at a time. If a competing explanation accounts for the effect, I retain the observation while rejecting or narrowing the vulnerability hypothesis.

## Evidence boundary

This illustrates economic attribution and false-positive rejection. It publishes no amounts, transactions or exploit sequence and claims no new technical result. This page describes a method; it is not independently verifiable execution evidence.
